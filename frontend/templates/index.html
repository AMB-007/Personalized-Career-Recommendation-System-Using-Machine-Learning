How Recommendations Are Generated

The system compares your assessment profile with career requirements and uses a validated XGBoost machine-learning model to estimate compatibility.

Recommendations are designed as exploratory guidance to help students discover options matching their natural strengths. Results do not constitute a fixed constraint on what you can pursue.

Responsible AI Principles
  • Zero usage of sensitive demographic attributes (no caste, religion, or gender bias).
  • Evaluates all 1,206 career candidates equally without hardcoded shortcuts.
  • Verified 96.18% Hit@1 recommendation ranking benchmark.
  • Complete data privacy with secure credential hashing.
